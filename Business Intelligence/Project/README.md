#  Project: Business Intelligence Analysis of Brazilian E-Commerce Customer Purchasing Behavior Using Olist Datasets 

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior in the Brazilian e-commerce platform using the Olist dataset. The main objective is to develop a complete business intelligence (BI) solution by integrating multiple datasets, including customers, orders, order items, and products.

The project applies data preparation techniques using Alteryx Designer, including data cleaning, transformation, and integration, to ensure data quality and consistency. The processed data is then visualized using Microsoft Power BI to generate interactive dashboards and meaningful insights. 

Through various analyses such as sales trends, product category performance, regional distribution, delivery performance, and customer segmentation, the project aims to support data-driven decision-making and improve business performance.

---

## 🎓 Reflection

This project has provided valuable experience in applying business intelligence concepts to real-world data. It enhanced understanding of how raw data from multiple sources can be transformed into meaningful insights through data integration, cleaning, and visualization processes.

During the project, several technical challenges were encountered, particularly in data integration and forecasting due to inconsistent data and limited historical records. These challenges highlighted the importance of data quality and careful data preprocessing before conducting analysis.

The use of tools such as Alteryx Designer and Microsoft Power BI also improved technical skills in workflow design, data transformation, and dashboard development. In addition, the project demonstrated how analytical techniques such as segmentation and trend analysis can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and business performance.

Overall, the project strengthened problem-solving skills, critical thinking, and the ability to apply BI techniques to support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion

In conclusion, this project successfully developed a business intelligence solution to analyze Brazilian e-commerce data using the Olist dataset. By integrating and transforming multiple datasets, meaningful insights were generated regarding sales performance, customer behavior, and logistics efficiency.

The analysis revealed important findings such as strong revenue concentration in certain product categories, regional differences in customer distribution, and the impact of delivery performance on customer satisfaction. These insights can support business strategies such as market expansion, logistics optimization, and targeted marketing.

Despite some limitations, such as data quality issues and limited forecasting accuracy, the project demonstrates the effectiveness of BI tools in transforming large datasets into actionable insights. It highlights the importance of data-driven approaches in improving business performance and supporting future growth.
